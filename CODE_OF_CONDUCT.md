# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, practical place to collaborate on local audio tooling. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate clearly, assume good faith, and keep discussions focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep criticism specific and technical.
- Accept that compatibility work can be fragile and uncertain.
- Respect maintainer time by keeping issues and pull requests focused.
- Do not pressure others to work on bypasses, proprietary material, or unsafe disclosures.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting secrets, credentials, raw private captures, or proprietary material.
- Demanding support as if this were a commercial product.
- Asking contributors to bypass authentication, DRM, signatures, pairing, certificates, or access controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close issues and pull requests that violate this code of conduct or the project's safety policies. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from project spaces.
